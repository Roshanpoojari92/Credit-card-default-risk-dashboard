Dataset/README.md# 📊 Credit Card Default Risk Dashboard

An interactive **Microsoft Excel Dashboard** analyzing **30,000 credit card customers** to uncover the key factors that influence payment default. The project is built using **dynamic Excel formulas, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers**, with no hardcoded calculations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard helps identify customer segments with a higher probability of default by analyzing demographic information, credit limits, payment history, and repayment behavior. It enables business users to explore trends through an interactive interface and supports data-driven credit risk decisions.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* **Overall Default Rate:** **22.1%** of customers defaulted on their next payment.
* **Credit Limit Impact:** Default risk decreases significantly as credit limits increase, dropping from **36%** in the lowest credit tier to **14%** in the highest tier.
* **Payment History Matters Most:** Customers with **3+ months of payment delay** have a **72% default rate**, compared to only **14%** for customers who consistently pay on time.
* **Age Analysis:** Younger customers showed a higher tendency to default compared to older age groups.
* **Repayment Behavior:** Customers who missed recent payments were consistently more likely to default than those with regular repayment patterns.
